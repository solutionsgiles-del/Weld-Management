## 1. Base Metal & Thickness Limits (Essential Variable)
* **Base Material:** AWS D1.6 Group A & B Austenitic Stainless Steels (e.g., 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 317L, 321, 347, UNS S30400, S31600)
* **Material Group:** Group I & II Prequalified Carbon & Low-Alloy Steels
* **Thickness Range Qualified:** t1: t1 = 16 Ga to 1/4 in [1.5 mm to 6 mm] max
* **Diameter Limits:** Plate, Structural Shapes & Tubular (within code limits)

---

## 2. Joint Design & Geometry
* **Joint Designation:** `B-L1a` (Groove / Butt Joint)
* **Diagram Classification:** `v_groove`
* **Applicable Joint Types:** Butt Joint (Single-V Groove), Corner Joint
* **Groove Angle:** 45° / 60°
* **Root Opening (R):** 0 to 1/8 in [0-3 mm]
* **Root Face (f):** 0 to 1/8 in [0 - 3 mm]
* **Backing / Root Preparation:** Steel Backing or Backgouged to sound metal

---

## 3. Filler Metal & Shielding Gas (Essential Variable)
* **AWS Filler Specification:** AWS A5.9
* **AWS Classification:** ER308L / ER316L Stainless Filler Rod
* **Electrode Diameter(s):** 1/16 in [1.6 mm], 3/32 in [2.4 mm], 1/8 in [3.2 mm]
* **Deposit Thickness Max:** Max 1/8 in [3 mm] per pass
* **Shielding Gas Composition:** 100% Argon (AWS A5.32 SG-A) + 100% Ar Backing Purge
* **Gas Flow Rate:** 15 - 22 CFH (7 - 10 L/min); Purge: 10 - 15 CFH
* **Submerged Arc Flux:** N/A
* **Power Source Type:** Constant Current (CC) High-Frequency Inverter
* **Current & Polarity:** DCEN (Direct Current Electrode Negative)

---

## 4. Position & Thermal Parameters (Essential Variable)
* **Qualified Positions:** 1G, 2G, 3G (Vertical Up), 4G, 1F, 2F, 3F, 4F
* **Progression:** Vertical-Up
* **Minimum Preheat Temperature:** Preheat: None required except moisture removal (>= 60°F [15°C]); Max Interpass Temp <= 350°F [175°C]
* **Maximum Interpass Temperature:** 500°F [260°C] Max
* **Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT):** None Required (AWS Prequalified)

---

## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root Pass (Pass 1) | GTAW | 3/32 in | DCEN | 85 - 125 A | 11 - 14 V | 4 - 6 ipm | 100% Ar purge (O2 < 50 ppm); keyhole root fusion; no preheat |
| Fill & Cap (Passes 2-N) | GTAW | 3/32 in | DCEN | 110 - 145 A | 12 - 15 V | 5 - 8 ipm | Stringer passes; clean stainless wire brush; interpass <= 350°F |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.6/D1.6M:2017 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, Figure 5.4. Process: GTAW. 2% Lanthanated or Ceriated tungsten (EWLa-2). Gas lens nozzle. Back-purging mandatory until at least 6mm weld deposited.
