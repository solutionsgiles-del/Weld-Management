## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pass 1 (Root & Fill 1) | SAW | 3/32 in | DCEP | 280 - 350 A | 28 - 31 V | 14 - 18 ipm | Submerged flux depth 20 mm; flux clean and dry |
| Pass 2-N (Cap) | SAW | 1/8 in | DCEP | 320 - 420 A | 29 - 33 V | 15 - 20 ipm | Self-peeling slag; interpass <= 350°F [175°C] |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.6/D1.6M:2017 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, Figure 5.4. Process: SAW. Maintain neutral flux depth 20 - 30 mm. Recover unconsumed flux with vacuum. No vertical welding permitted.
