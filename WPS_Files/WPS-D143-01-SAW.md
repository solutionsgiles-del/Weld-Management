## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root (Pass 1) | SAW | 3/32 in | DCEP | 320 - 380 A | 28 - 30 V | 14 - 18 ipm | Deep penetration into root or backing strip; verify seam alignment |
| Fill (Passes 2-N) | SAW | 5/32 in | DCEP | 420 - 540 A | 30 - 33 V | 16 - 22 ipm | High deposition rate; automatic travel track |
| Cap (Pass Final) | SAW | 5/32 in | DCEP | 380 - 480 A | 29 - 32 V | 15 - 20 ipm | Flat smooth crown profile; peel slag cleanly |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS 14.3/14.3M:2010 Clause 7.5, Table 4, Table 6, Table 7M & Annex A (Figures A.1, A.3, A.5). Process: SAW. Flux depth 25 - 35 mm above arc. Flux must be stored heated at 120°C. Machine ground clamped solidly to prevent arc blow.
