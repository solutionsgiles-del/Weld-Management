### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.6/D1.6M:2017 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, Figure 5.4. Process: SMAW. Maintain low heat input (< 1.5 kJ/mm). Store electrodes in heated cabinet at 120°C. Stainless steel wire brush only.
