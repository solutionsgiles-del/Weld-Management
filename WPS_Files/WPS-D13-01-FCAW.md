* **Supporting PQR:** PREQUALIFIED (AWS Clause 5 Exemption)  
* **Status:** Approved for Production  
* **Governing AWS Code:** AWS D1.3/D1.3M:2018 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 1.2, 1.3, Figures 5.1A/5.1B

## 1. Base Metal & Thickness Limits (Essential Variable)
* **Base Material:** AWS D1.3 Group I, II, III, IV (ASTM A36, A653, A1008, A1011 Sheet Steels)
* **Material Group:** Group I & II Prequalified Carbon & Low-Alloy Steels
* **Thickness Range Qualified:** t1: Sheet steel t1 = 18 Ga (0.0478 in) to 7 Ga (0.1793 in) / 3/16 in [5 mm] max
* **Diameter Limits:** Plate, Structural Shapes & Tubular (within code limits)

---

## 2. Joint Design & Geometry
* **Joint Designation:** `B-P1a / B-P1c` (Groove / Butt Joint)
* **Diagram Classification:** `v_groove`
* **Applicable Joint Types:** Butt Joint (Single-V Groove), Corner Joint
* **Groove Angle:** 45° / 60°
* **Root Opening (R):** 0 to 1/8 in [0-3 mm]
* **Root Face (f):** 0 to 1/8 in [0 - 3 mm]
* **Backing / Root Preparation:** Steel Backing or Backgouged to sound metal

---

## 3. Filler Metal & Shielding Gas (Essential Variable)
* **AWS Filler Specification:** AWS A5.20
* **AWS Classification:** E71T-11 / E71T-14 Self-Shielded or E71T-1M
* **Electrode Diameter(s):** 0.035 in [0.9 mm], 0.045 in [1.2 mm]
* **Deposit Thickness Max:** Max 1/8 in [3 mm] per pass
* **Shielding Gas Composition:** None (Self-Shielded FCAW-S) or 100% CO2
* **Gas Flow Rate:** None / 35 CFH
* **Submerged Arc Flux:** N/A (Internal Flux Core)
* **Power Source Type:** Constant Voltage (CV) Heavy-Duty Power Source
* **Current & Polarity:** DCEP (Gas-Shielded) / DCEN (Self-Shielded)

---

## 4. Position & Thermal Parameters (Essential Variable)
* **Qualified Positions:** 1G, 2G, 3G (Vertical Up), 4G, 1F, 2F, 3F, 4F
* **Progression:** Vertical-Up
* **Minimum Preheat Temperature:** Ambient temperature >= 32°F [0°C] (removal of moisture required)
* **Maximum Interpass Temperature:** 500°F [260°C] Max
* **Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT):** None Required (AWS Prequalified)

---

## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pass 1 (Single Pass) | FCAW | 0.035 in | DCEN | 110 - 150 A | 17 - 20 V | 10 - 16 ipm | High speed travel; smooth arc without spatter |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.3/D1.3M:2018 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 1.2, 1.3, Figures 5.1A/5.1B. Process: FCAW. DCEN polarity for FCAW-S. Chipping and wire brush between all passes.
