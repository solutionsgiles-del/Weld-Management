## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pass 1 (Single Pass) | SMAW | 3/32 in | DCEP | 60 - 85 A | 20 - 22 V | 8 - 12 ipm | Rapid travel to avoid burn-through on thin sheet steel |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.3/D1.3M:2018 Clause 5, Table 5.1; Tables 1.2, 1.3, Figures 5.1A/5.1B. Process: SMAW. Touch strike carefully; maintain tight arc to avoid excessive melt-through on light gauge sheet.
