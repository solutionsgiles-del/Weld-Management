## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root (Pass 1) | GMAW | 0.035 in | DCEP | 140 - 175 A | 19 - 22 V | 8 - 12 ipm | Stringer pass; sound root fusion; backgouge root where applicable |
| Fill (Passes 2-N) | GMAW | 0.045 in | DCEP | 200 - 260 A | 25 - 28 V | 11 - 16 ipm | Spray arc transfer; clean interpass with wire wheel |
| Cap (Pass Final) | GMAW | 0.045 in | DCEP | 180 - 230 A | 24 - 27 V | 9 - 14 ipm | Crown reinforcement 1/16 to 1/8 in [1.5 - 3 mm] |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS 14.3/14.3M:2010 Clause 7.5, Table 4, Table 6, Table 7M & Annex A (Figures A.1, A.3, A.5). Process: GMAW. Maintain wire stickout 15 - 20 mm. Shield weld pool from drafts > 5 mph. Clean nozzle every 3 passes.
