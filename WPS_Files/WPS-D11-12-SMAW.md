## 5. Electrical Parameters & Technique Pass Sequence

| Pass No. | Process | Filler Diameter | Current / Polarity | Amperage Range | Voltage Range | Travel Speed | Remarks / Technique |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root (Pass 1) | SMAW | 3/32 in | DCEP | 80 - 105 A | 21 - 23 V | 4 - 6 ipm | Stringer bead; sound root penetration; backgouge where required |
| Fill (Passes 2-N) | SMAW | 1/8 in | DCEP | 115 - 145 A | 22 - 25 V | 5 - 7 ipm | Split layers if groove width > 5/8 in [16 mm] |
| Cap (Pass Final) | SMAW | 5/32 in | DCEP | 140 - 165 A | 23 - 26 V | 5 - 8 ipm | Crown reinforcement <= 1/8 in [3 mm]; smooth toe transition |

### Technique & Workmanship Requirements
* **Stringer or Weave:** Stringer bead preferred; weave max width per code
* **Interpass Cleaning:** Power wire brush, chipping hammer & degrease
* **Peening:** Prohibited on root and final cover pass
* **Notes:** Prequalified per AWS D1.1:2020 Clause 5.4.2, Figure 5.2; Tables 5.5, 5.8. Process: SMAW. Bake electrodes at 250°C-350°C for 2h prior to issue. Maintain in portable 120°C warmers. Max weave width 2.5x core wire diameter.
